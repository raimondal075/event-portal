Questions

Frequently asked

Who can participate?

Students from 1st to 4th year. Some events may be limited to certain years; each event card shows who it's open to.

Which email should I use to create my account?

2nd, 3rd and 4th year students must use their institutional email. 1st year students can use a personal email if they don't have an institutional one yet.

Can I register for more than one event?

Yes. Events run in separate time slots, so you can join as many as you like. Each registration gets its own ticket ID.

Can I participate solo in FrostByte or the Innovation Lab?

Yes. Both events allow individual or team entries. Choose "Team" in the registration form to add a team name and members.

I forgot my password. What do I do?

Tap “Forgot password?” on the sign-in page and we'll email you a reset link. You can also ask the coordinators to send one.

What should I bring on the day?

Your college ID card and your ticket ID. For FrostByte and Aurora ArtFest, bring your own laptop if you have one.
